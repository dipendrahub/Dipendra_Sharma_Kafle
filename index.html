About

I'm a PhD candidate working at the intersection of information retrieval, knowledge graphs, and natural language processing, trying to build systems that don't just find the right entity, but can tell you why it's the right one.

Most days I'm somewhere between building retrieval-augmented pipelines for expert-finding, arguing with bias metrics in search systems.

Before the PhD, I was a research engineer building document-understanding pipelines in industry, and before that I was chasing structural-health-monitoring sensors around a lab in Weimar, Germany. I like problems that sit between messy real-world data and rigorous method. I like them even more when the result is something a non-expert can actually understand and use, whether that's in digital humanities or public policy.

Looking ahead, I want to go deeper into understanding foundational models themselves, not just building on top of them, and apply that understanding to new causes, healthcare being the one I'm most drawn to right now.

Outside the lab, I'm usually mid-podcast, planning the next trip, or negotiating with my own playlist algorithm over what counts as good music.
